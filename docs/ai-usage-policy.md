# AI Usage Policy

The Train-Game team may use generative AI to support research, planning, design, documentation, debugging, testing, and implementation. Team members remain responsible for every submitted artifact.

## Approved use

AI may be used to:

- brainstorm alternatives and questions;
- organize or revise team-authored material;
- summarize sources that the team can independently inspect;
- draft diagrams, tests, pseudocode, or starter code;
- explain unfamiliar concepts or error messages; and
- review work for omissions, clarity, or likely defects.

AI output is a draft or advisory input. It is not evidence that a requirement is satisfied or that code is correct.

## Required logging

Add an entry to `AI_LOG.md` when AI materially contributes to:

- project or system architecture;
- algorithms or data models;
- starter code or substantial code changes;
- requirements, policies, reports, or other structural content;
- market or competitor analysis; or
- final design decisions.

Basic syntax searches, autocomplete, spelling fixes, and formatting-only assistance do not require individual entries unless they materially change the work.

Each entry must identify the date, team member, tool and available model/version, prompt summary, output and intended use, and the human verification or revisions performed. Do not paste secrets, private personal data, proprietary credentials, or restricted course material into an AI tool.

## Human verification

Before accepting AI-assisted work, a team member must:

1. Compare it with assignment and project requirements.
2. Check factual claims against reliable primary sources when possible.
3. Review code for correctness, security, licensing, and maintainability.
4. Run relevant tests or manually verify behavior.
5. Rewrite material so it accurately reflects the team's own decisions.
6. Record those checks and changes in `AI_LOG.md`.

For research, cite the original source rather than the AI tool. AI must not invent survey results, customer evidence, citations, test results, or completed work.

## Attribution and ownership

The team will retain the repository AI log throughout the project and disclose AI use when required by the instructor or institution. The person who submits or merges AI-assisted work owns its accuracy. The team may reject or replace any output that cannot be adequately verified.
